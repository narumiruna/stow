App Lock is app-access protection, not additional encryption. Do not delete the app/library or silently reset the lock to recover from authentication failures. Keep the gate closed and retry system authentication or the device passcode. If the gate cannot be enforced, stop distribution and fix it; reverting to a build without the gate is not a security-preserving rollback. Preview fetching can remain Off without deleting saved URLs or metadata.
